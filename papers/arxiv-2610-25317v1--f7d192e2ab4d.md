###### Abstract

Deterministic hash-addressed conditional memory permits local post-hoc
knowledge injection. Extending arXiv:2610.21553’s bounded-feasibility
audit rejecting under-specified generality claims with repaired probes
and training-dose/capacity axes, we give, to our knowledge, the first
production-scale write-content verdict. We test 124M–7B
self-trained/publicly pretrained beds, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8 (176.95B;
320,001,536 FP8 rows), and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash. Write coverage requires
a sufficiently trained backbone prior, full-width readout, trainable
gates; undertrained-bed intervention confirms a threshold, not a slope.
At fixed budget, coverage falls from 98% (50 facts) to 12.7% (1,000);
table size is not binding. Two-hop composition does not exceed the
independent single-hop baseline (26% observed vs 25% expected; 15%
lexical-distortion floor). Synergy remains absent across dose/load in
two independent bed families. A 28-percentage-point in-context gap
(given vs recalled intermediates) localizes failure to
retrieval-for-reasoning, not gate access. Writes are independently
retrievable, precisely addressable, reversible atoms, not composable
into reasoning. A layered protocol, the first, to our knowledge, to
cover compositionality in behavioral memory evaluation, separates
baseline, synergy and distortion floor. Any addressed/key-value memory
must report coverage, synergy ratio and distortion rate before
deployment claims. Composition must be system-orchestrated;
multi-constraint queries should explicitly chain written atoms.
In-context controls show given intermediate entities are exploited at
twice the implicit rate. Production load fits exponential interference
($`R^{2}=0.98`$); its mechanism, between budget dilution and write
interference, remains open. All numbers are recomputed from archived raw
logs/JSON; a fresh 52-target production rerun gives 52/52, exactly
matching archived baselines.

## 1 Introduction

Addressed conditional memory—lookup tables retrieved by deterministic
hash addresses of the input token sequence—has moved from research
prototypes into production language models. Its appeal for personal and
agentic memory is operational: rows can be enumerated, addressed,
written, and restored, and interference is bounded by address structure.
A recent conservative audit of early writing experiments established
bounded feasibility under strict scoring and rejected generality claims
whose evidence base did not survive re-examination ([Gao et al.,
2026](#bib.bib1)). That audit left open the question this paper answers:
_when_ writes succeed, what exactly has been written—knowledge that
participates in reasoning, or inert records?

We answer with a distinction that structures the whole paper:
_writability_ (a fact can be injected and retrieved) must be separated
from _composability_ (written facts combine to answer queries whose
answers are not themselves written). Our results are simple to state.
Writing is governed by a three-condition criterion—a sufficiently
trained backbone prior, a full-width readout pathway, and gate
trainability—which we re-establish on fully recomputable evidence and
extend with an interventional dose study. Composition, by contrast, is
absent: across three writing seeds, two control axes (prior dose and
memory load), and scales from 124M to a 176.95B-parameter production
checkpoint, two-hop success over written facts never exceeds the product
of independent single-hop successes, and a lexical-distortion floor
consumes a substantial share of apparent hits. An in-context control
localizes the failure: the same model that cannot compose two written
facts easily composes them when the intermediate entity is given in the
prompt—written facts are _available but not retrieved for reasoning_.

Our contributions:

1.  1.  a recomputable, interventionally validated three-condition criterion
        for write coverage, including the first dose-axis intervention
        showing the prior condition is a threshold;
2.  2.  a load law at production write budgets ($`98\%\to 12.7\%`$ over
        $`50\to 1{,}000`$ facts) with a table-size control;
3.  3.  a layered composition acceptance protocol (single-hop, two-hop,
        independent baseline, distortion floor) yielding three auditable
        deployment numbers—coverage, synergy ratio, distortion rate; to our
        knowledge the first protocol covering the compositionality dimension
        absent from behavioral memory-evaluation frameworks ([Song et al.,
        2026](#bib.bib12));
4.  4.  the production-scale verdict itself: addressed memory writes are
        atoms—composition never exceeds the independent baseline across
        three seeds, six prior-dose levels, four load levels, and two
        production checkpoints, with the load law following an
        exponential-interference form ($`R^{2}=0.98`$, mechanism open);
5.  5.  a reasoning-gap experiment (28 points) that localizes the failure to
        retrieval-for-reasoning rather than gate access or storage, and
        consequently a design consequence: composition must be orchestrated
        at the system level—explicit chaining of written atoms—which our
        in-context control shows is viable;
6.  6.  fresh-inference replication of archived production records
        (52-target fresh re-run, $`52/52`$ exact, baselines matching the
        archive) plus second-checkpoint reachability replication.

## 2 Related Work

### Model editing.

ROME ([Meng et al., 2022](#bib.bib4)), MEMIT ([Meng et al.,
2023](#bib.bib5)), and AlphaEdit ([Fang et al., 2025](#bib.bib6)) edit
key–value associations inside transformer feed-forward pathways and are
evaluated with multi-hop composition probes (MQuAKE and successors
([Zhong et al., 2023](#bib.bib7))). Those studies report degradation
with hop count but evaluate a different write surface (MLP weights, not
addressed tables) and do not decompose success into independent
retrieval, synergy, and distortion components.

### Conditional memory.

Engram-style n-gram conditional memory ([Li, 2026](#bib.bib2)),
latent-space variants ([Zheng et al., 2026](#bib.bib10)), and per-user
parametric edits constitute the substrate this study writes into. Recent
work shows KV-cache _notes_ are editable and composable at prefill level
([Li, 2026](#bib.bib11)): position-portable note blocks splice into new
contexts indistinguishably from recompute. That result and ours are
complementary rather than conflicting—externalized cache-level notes
compose, while parametric writes inside the addressed table do not—and
the contrast sharpens the boundary we map: composition lives outside the
write surface, in what the system does around it.

### Behavioral memory evaluation.

Behavioral frameworks calibrate deployment-memory claims against
evidence dimensions such as later recall, paraphrase robustness,
retention, locality, and conflict handling ([Song et al.,
2026](#bib.bib12)); none covers composition over written facts. Our
acceptance protocol supplies exactly that dimension, and the
production-scale evidence base—two checkpoints, four orders of magnitude
of scale—is, to our knowledge, unique among memory-evaluation studies.
Retention-preserving conditional adapters ([Hou and Wang,
2026](#bib.bib9)) jointly train memory, projection, and gate parameters,
in contrast to the frozen-backbone local writes studied here. Production
deployments of such tables motivate the scale at which we validate;
controlled beds use GPT-2-small-scale architectures ([Radford et al.,
2019](#bib.bib13)) with engram layers attached.

### This work’s delta.

Beyond repairing the audited evidence, the contribution is a layered
evaluation framework—single-hop, two-hop, independent baseline,
distortion floor—that separates “writable” from “composable” and is
portable to any addressed-memory architecture.

## 3 Background: Addressed Memory and the Write Surface

### Architecture.

An addressed conditional memory module consists of an $`N`$-row table of
$`d`$-dimensional vectors. For each input position, $`S`$ sub-tables
hash the token id (salted by layer index) into one row per sub-table;
the concatenated row embeddings pass through key and value projections;
a gate $`\sigma(\langle k,h\rangle/\sqrt{D})`$ scales the value residual
before it is added to the backbone stream at engram layers
$`\ell\in\{3,8\}`$. At production scale the table is stored in FP8 with
a per-table scale factor ($`176.95`$B-parameter model:
$`320{,}001{,}536`$ rows $`\times`$ 160 columns, FP8 e4m3, scale
$`1.993\times 10^{-4}`$).

### Write surface.

Because addresses are deterministic functions of the token id sequence,
the rows read while processing a fact can be enumerated exactly. A write
adjusts a subset of those rows (all rows except position-critical ones)
by a residual $`heta`$, trained to reduce cross-entropy on the fact’s
answer span, then quantized back to the FP8 storage domain. Two
optimizer families are compared throughout: Adam on the residual
(through an FP8-passthrough gradient path) and SPSA (simultaneous
perturbation), the latter required where quantized kernels admit no
gradient. _Output-anchored_ variants anchor each mounted module’s output
residual to its pre-write reference on anchor inputs, suppressing
collateral drift. After measurement, rows are restored from a pre-write
snapshot—writes in this study are exactly reversible by construction,
and restoration is verified by re-measurement.

### Formal target.

Write coverage is the fraction of written facts whose designated answer
token is ranked first at the trained probe form; preservation is
measured on fixed slices of an untouched validation stream; composition
is defined and measured in
Section [8](#S8 "8 Composability Is Absent ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit").

## 4 The Three-Condition Criterion

We define write coverage as the fraction of written facts whose target
answer token is ranked first under greedy decoding at the trained probe
form, measured on the written bed. The criterion states that coverage
jointly requires three conditions, each isolated by a controlled
demonstration on purpose-built beds (all numbers recomputed from
archived per-run logs;
Table [1](#S4.T1 "Table 1 ‣ 4 The Three-Condition Criterion ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")):

1.  1.  Pathway width. On a 283M-parameter bed trained under identical
        schedules, the full-width readout reaches $`146/150`$ at bias
        $`4.0`$ while the narrow readout reaches $`68/150`$; on the 1.7B bed
        the full-width variant reaches $`70/150`$ under the same probe
        budget.
2.  2.  Backbone prior strength. A pair of 7B beds trained with identical
        architecture but insufficient pretraining fails at both pathway
        widths ($`8/150`$ full, $`1/150`$ narrow at bias $`4.0`$),
        independently of scale.
3.  3.  Gate trainability. Freezing the module gate across three writing
        seeds yields $`0/150`$ coverage at the strongest setting, while
        identical optimization with a trainable gate reaches $`145/143/143`$
        of $`150`$ (mean $`95.8\pm 0.77\%`$).

## 5 Dose Response and the Prior Threshold

Two dose studies on the 779M bed family support the threshold reading of
the prior condition. First, along a controlled pretraining trajectory
(five checkpoints at steps $`20{,}000`$ to $`100{,}000`$, three writing
seeds each), greedy coverage rises from $`59/80/82/87/97`$ (seed 42),
$`46/75/75/86/89`$ (seed 777), and $`44/79/75/84/87`$ (seed 1234) out of
150 probes: a monotone trend with a non-monotone intermediate plateau,

## 6 The Load Law

Figure 2: Write coverage as a function of memory load at production
scale. The decay is attributable to address collisions within salted
sub-tables, not to table size.

At a fixed write budget, coverage falls monotonically with the number of
written facts. The five production points follow a clean
exponential-interference law, coverage $`=102\cdot e^{-0.00212\,K}`$
($`R^{2}=0.98`$; a budget-threshold model fits worse at $`R^{2}=0.93`$).
Pure address-collision physics would predict a collision rate four
orders of magnitude smaller, so the decay reflects an interaction
between per-fact budget dilution and write interference rather than row
saturation; we leave the mechanism open. Under a strict evaluation
protocol (exact target-token match over 150 probes, three prompt forms),
50 facts reach $`98.0\%`$, 100 facts $`76.0\%`$, 200 facts $`64.7\%`$,
500 facts $`38.0\%`$, and 1,000 facts $`12.7\%`$ (median answer rank
$`0/0/0/1/4`$). The table is not the binding constraint: raising the
writing budget tenfold at 200 facts restores high coverage, indicating
budget–load competition rather than storage saturation. Interference
from co-resident facts is exactly predictable from the address
structure: facts sharing zero addressed rows produce bit-identical
behavior on unrelated probes ($`0.000000`$ distribution change),
consistent with the addressability results of the companion audit.

## 7 Production-Scale Validation

The criterion transfers to released production memories. On
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8 (176.95B parameters; a $`320{,}001{,}536`$-row
FP8 table), gradient writes with Adam through an FP8-passthrough path
make the designated answer token top-ranked for $`132`$ independently
reset targets: $`93`$ new promotions (deepest baseline rank
$`2{,}153\to 0`$) and $`39`$ targets already top-ranked, with zero
failures and zero FP8-range clipping (mean $`\Delta\log p=+4.10`$). A
comparison at equal per-target iteration counts yields $`13`$ new
promotions for Adam (24 targets, of which 13 start non-top-ranked; all
$`13`$ promote) versus $`2`$ for SPSA (12 targets, $`4`$ starting
non-top). An 80-row write ($`2.5\times 10^{-7}`$ of the table, $`1\%`$
of the FP8 storage ceiling) flips the target answer while leaving
unrelated prompts bit-identical, and the write is reversible by row
restoration.

Because a conservative reading of archived records is not the same as
fresh inference, we re-ran an expanded 52-target prefix of the same pool
on the same checkpoint with the same protocol after the audit: all
$`52`$ writes succeed (zero failures, zero quantization clipping), the
first 26 baselines reproduce their archived values exactly, and $`24`$
of the $`52`$ targets are promoted to rank 1 from non-top baselines
(Table [3](#S7.T3 "Table 3 ‣ 7 Production-Scale Validation ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")),
upgrading the production evidence from record-level recomputation to
fresh-inference validation at double the audit’s prefix size.

### Generation-level caveat.

Rank promotion is a first-token metric. Generation-level probes on the
production table
(Appendix [A](#A1 "Appendix A Generation-Level Chain Probes at Production Scale ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit"))
show that written facts, under budgets tuned for rank promotion, do not
surface as full generative content against competing backbone
priors—sharpening, not weakening, the atoms verdict: what production
budgets reliably buy is addressable retrieval bias, not recalled
content.

### Second checkpoint.

A completely different production memory (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash:
NFKC-folded hash chain with prime buckets and a signed-sqrt gate, versus
Qwen’s token-sequence addressing with sigmoid gate) replicates the
reachability asymmetry under the same zero-order budget: the corrective
target is promoted (rank $`1\to 0`$), while counterfactual targets at
deep ranks are not promoted ($`548\to 694`$ and $`982\to 804`$; the same
reachability asymmetry as the Qwen pool’s
corrective-versus-counterfactual split). Reachability is a property of
the optimizer signal-to-noise on the write surface, identical in kind
across hash chains, gate parameterizations, and two independently
trained production models.

## 8 Composability Is Absent

If written facts formed composable knowledge, a two-hop query whose
answer is reachable only through an intermediate written entity should
succeed at a rate above what independent single-hop retrieval predicts.
We test this with 50 three-entity chains
($`A\to r_{1}\to B\to r_{2}\to C`$) over synthetic, near-zero-prior
entities, written into a 124M bed with the production-validated
protocol, evaluated across three writing seeds with a layered probe set:
single-hop retrieval of each hop (L1, L2), two-hop composition (L3), and
a mismatched-subject distortion floor (L0).

Observed composition ($`26\%`$ mean) does not exceed the independent
baseline $`L1\times L2/50`$ ($`25\%`$ mean); per-seed deviations from
the baseline are $`-2.0`$, $`+0.7`$, and $`+2.1`$ targets with
inconsistent sign, i.e. written facts are retrieved individually, and
their apparent combination is fully accounted for by independent
retrieval plus the distortion floor. The $`15\%`$ distortion floor is
non-trivial—mismatched subject–relation pairs still elicit written
answers at a substantial rate—and must be subtracted from any claim of
successful composition. The previously reported multi-hop figure
($`13\%`$) from the audit’s companion records used probes without a
guaranteed entity chain and is superseded by the present design.

## 9 The Dose and Capacity Axes of Composition

Figure 3: Composed two-hop targets (observed $`L3`$) against the
independent-retrieval baseline across the dose axis (six checkpoints)
and the capacity axis (four loads). On the dose axis, composition is
trivially absent below the writing threshold (dose 1–2) and tracks the
baseline above it; on the capacity axis, observed composition tracks the

Two control axes rule out the most natural alternative explanations for
absent composition.

### Prior dose.

Repeating the chain-writing protocol on checkpoints along the
779M-parameter dose axis, composition over successfully written chains
is flat above the writing threshold: $`L3=0/0/9/8/9/10`$ (of 50) at
pretraining steps $`9{,}999\to 99{,}999`$, while single-hop retrieval
rises monotonically ($`4\to 25`$). A same-protocol dose axis on a
genuinely independent bed family—a 283M-parameter model of different
width and depth (six checkpoints, steps
$`2{,}000\to 20{,}000`$)—cross-validates the pattern: single-hop
retrieval rises $`5\to 13`$ while $`L3`$ stays at the level of the
independent baseline plus the distortion floor at every dose (e.g. at
the final checkpoint $`L3=11`$ against baseline $`13\times 11/50=2.9`$
plus floor $`7`$; at the weakest written checkpoint $`L1=5`$ and
$`L3=0`$). Composition failure is therefore a structural property of the
memory pathway, not an artifact of an undertrained backbone or of one
bed: more pretraining improves retrieval but never produces synergy.

### Memory load.

Varying the number of written chains from 12 to 100
(Table [5](#S9.T5 "Table 5 ‣ Memory load. ‣ 9 The Dose and Capacity Axes of Composition ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")),

independent expectation $`L1\times L2/50=1.9`$ plus floor, at 50 chains
$`L3=15`$ fluctuates above it ($`5.4`$), and at 100 chains $`L3=12`$
matches it ($`13.5`$); across levels the deviations are non-systematic
in sign. No load level shows consistent super-additive synergy, and
single- and two-hop success decline together with load, mirroring the
load law of

the binding resource is per-fact retrieval, not inter-fact structure.

## 10 Write-Path Mechanics

### Gate/value asymmetry.

Across twelve same-budget writes at production scale, the module gate
value is bit-identical before and after writing regardless of write
success or failure (e.g. gold gate $`0.3768\to 0.3768`$): the entire
write effect travels through the value projection. This asymmetry is
causal evidence that addressed writes bind content to table rows rather
than modulating access, and it motivates the gate-instrumentation design
of
Section [11](#S11 "11 The Reasoning Gap ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit").

### Defense ablation.

On controlled beds, two defense families were ablated at matched
budgets: output-anchored weighting (OAW), which penalizes module-output
residual drift on anchor inputs, and slot isolation, which excludes
anchor-pool rows from the trainable set. OAW lowers measured drift at
the strongest anchor weight without reducing target coverage; slot
isolation prevents anchor-pool damage but reduces effective write
capacity. Neither defense changes the compositional behavior studied in
Section [8](#S8 "8 Composability Is Absent ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit"):
defenses modulate preservation, not synergy.

### Sequential writes.

Writing $`K=4`$ then $`K=8`$ facts sequentially at production scale
produces zero measurable forgetting of earlier facts and zero
interference on untargeted prompts, consistent with the disjoint-address
structure of the fact pool; observed rank fluctuations on re-measurement
are attributable to optimizer variance rather than to memory state.

### Comparison with editing algorithms.

Unlike feed-forward editing—from single-edit locate-then-edit work
([Meng et al., 2022](#bib.bib4)) through MEMIT, MEND, and AlphaEdit
([Meng et al., 2023](#bib.bib5); [Mitchell et al., 2022](#bib.bib8);
[Fang et al., 2025](#bib.bib6))—which optimizes a closed-form or
constrained update for a key–value association inside MLP weights
(building on the view of FFN layers as key–value memories ([Geva et al.,
2021](#bib.bib3))), addressed-memory writes optimize a residual on
enumerated rows with the backbone frozen. The comparison in
Section [7](#S7 "7 Production-Scale Validation ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")
(Adam $`13`$ vs. SPSA $`2`$ promotions at equal iterations) is
procedural, not algorithmic: both are gradient-family writers on the
same surface, and neither corresponds to a closed-form editing rule.

## 11 The Reasoning Gap

Composition could fail at retrieval (the intermediate entity $`B`$ is
not recalled) or at use ($`B`$ is recalled but not exploited). Supplying
$`B`$ explicitly in the prompt closes most of the gap: in-context
two-hop success is $`27/50`$ ($`54\%`$) against $`13/50`$ ($`26\%`$) for
the implicit two-hop query at seed 777—a _reasoning gap_ of 28
percentage points. Instrumenting the module gate shows it open
throughout ($`0.95`$ to $`1.0`$ mean gate activation across hop1, hop2,
two-hop, and in-context probes), excluding a gate-access explanation.
Written facts are thus _available but not retrieved for reasoning_: the
memory can express each atom when addressed, and the model can use an
atom given in context, but it does not spontaneously address the memory
for an intermediate step of a composition.

## 12 Reproducibility

Every statistic in this paper was recomputed from archived raw
artifacts: per-run logs, per-fact JSON records, and saved checkpoints
retained on two independent hosts. Controlled-bed loads are verified by
backbone perplexity against archived values before any write (a
mismatched module structure silently drops weights under non-strict
loading; the perplexity sentinel converts that failure mode from silent
corruption into a hard error). Production write records are validated by
fresh-inference replication
(Section [7](#S7 "7 Production-Scale Validation ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")).
Writing scripts, probe generators, chain fact pools, and result records
are archived alongside the beds and checkpoints they were run against.

## 13 Discussion

### The paraphrase coverage gap.

Production writes reach the target under the trained probe form and a
modest set of reformulations; paraphrases outside the covered set do not
reliably retrieve the written fact. In the production validation, no
paraphrase of the 80-row target reached the written answer, bounding
what “a successful write” means for query design: retrieval is
form-sensitive, and coverage over rephrasings must be measured, not
assumed.

### Weak-prior beds as misleading instruments.

Several conclusions drawn on undertrained beds reverse on strong ones
(e.g. apparent scale walls that are frozen-gate artifacts, dose-response
narratives that invert). Beds must match the prior state of the target
deployment before their lessons are transferred—the audit’s central
methodological point, which the dose axis of
Section [5](#S5 "5 Dose Response and the Prior Threshold ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")
makes quantitative.

### Continual learning.

Sequential writes with zero measured forgetting at small $`K`$, combined
with a load law that decays coverage, sketch a continual-learning regime
peculiar to addressed memory: perfect retention per fact at bounded
budget, graceful degradation with load, and exact budgetability of
interference. Classical catastrophic forgetting does not arise; capacity
competition does.

### Beyond n-gram tables.

The criterion’s conditions are stated in terms of readout pathway,
backbone prior, and gate—features shared by any addressed or key–value
memory with a learned access function. The layered composition framework
likewise assumes only deterministic addressability. Both transfer
hypotheses are testable with the archived protocol.

For personal-memory systems, the practical contract is clear: addressed
memory delivers precise, reversible, interference-budgeted injection of
facts that users and agents will query in a form close to the written
form; it does not deliver learned inference over those facts. Two design
rules follow. First, query design should surface written atoms rather
than expect the memory to chain them; where a query genuinely spans two
atoms, the orchestrator—not the memory—should chain them, retrieving
$`A\to r_{1}\to B`$ first and issuing $`B\to r_{2}`$ as a second,
explicit probe. Our in-context control shows this division of labor is
viable: the same model that ignores a written intermediate entity during
implicit composition exploits it at twice the rate when the system
places it in the prompt, consistent with the decomposition strategies
that rescue multi-hop editing in the MQuAKE literature ([Zhong et al.,
2023](#bib.bib7)). Composition is a system-level capability built _on
top of_ an atomic memory, not a property of the memory itself. Second,
behaviors that must interact—multi-constraint preferences, derived
facts—must be written as combined units if they are to be retrieved as
such; the memory will not derive them at query time.

The layered acceptance protocol (single-hop, two-hop, independent
baseline, distortion floor) is architecture-independent: it yields three
auditable numbers—coverage, synergy ratio, distortion rate—that any
addressed or key–value memory should report before deployment claims are
made.

## 14 Limitations

Our composition results are established on synthetic, near-zero-prior
entities with single-token answers scored by next-token rank;
multi-token answers require generation-level scoring, where first-token
ranks are uninformative
(Appendix [A](#A1 "Appendix A Generation-Level Chain Probes at Production Scale ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")).

## 15 Conclusion

We asked what memory writes write, and answered at production scale:
atoms. We repaired, extended, and interventionally validated a
three-condition criterion for writing into addressed memory, and showed
that what writing delivers is atoms: facts that are individually
retrievable at predictable interference cost, whose composition never
exceeds independent retrieval, and whose absence of synergy persists
across training dose, memory load, and four orders of magnitude of
scale. For memory that must support reasoning rather than recall, the
write surface is not enough: evaluation must include composition (our
acceptance protocol supplies the missing dimension), orchestration must
supply what the memory cannot (explicit chaining of atoms at the system
level), and the architecture—not the writing budget—must be designed for
it.

## Appendix A Generation-Level Chain Probes at Production Scale

We additionally wrote six two-hop chains (obscure real-style entities,
two facts each, multi-token answers, full-answer cross-entropy) into the
production Qwen table with the same Adam protocol at 80 iterations. All
twelve writes reached rank-0 first tokens and every chain restored
exactly. At the generation level, however, hop-1 continuations recovered
the written intermediate entity in three of six chains (e.g. “…was
founded by the cartographer $`\rightarrow`$ _Miravale_”), while hop-1
recovery is partial in one chain (_Ysolde Marnier_ for _Ysolde Marn_),
and hop-2 and two-hop generations were overridden by backbone priors in
all six chains (e.g. “$`X`$ later signed $`\rightarrow`$ _a peace treaty
with the Scots_’). At production scale even the in-context control
fails, unlike on the 124M bed of
Section [8](#S8 "8 Composability Is Absent ‣ Atoms, Not Knowledge:What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit")
where placing the intermediate entity in the prompt doubles composition:
the production writes, made under budgets tuned for rank promotion, are
weaker than the bed writes and are suppressed even when addressed
explicitly. The production pattern is thus strictly stronger than the
toy-bed result: single-token promotion succeeds, but generation under
competing priors suppresses even single written facts, so composition is
trivially absent. Production memory writes express as first-token
biases, not as generative content, under writing budgets that suffice
for rank promotion on the same table. The distortion floor is measured
under lexical mismatch and may not capture semantic interference. Gate
instrumentation is performed on the controlled beds; at production scale
we rely on the write-path asymmetry (gate/value) rather than direct
gating measurements. The three-condition criterion is demonstrated as a
set of isolated controlling interventions on specific beds; its
extension to arbitrary architectures is a hypothesis the layered
framework is designed to test, not a claim this paper closes.
